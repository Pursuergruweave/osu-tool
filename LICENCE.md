## 🧩 About

**osu! Tool** is a comprehensive overlay and training suite for osu! on PC featuring an in-game menu, accuracy tracker, hit error meter, replay analyzer, skin helper, and practice utilities.

Based on community tools like **osu!lazer overlays**, **tosu**, and **StreamCompanion**.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Tools
- In-Game Overlay — live stats without leaving the song
- Accuracy Tracker — real-time accuracy and combo display
- Hit Error Meter — visualize early/late timing per hit
- Replay Analyzer — review UR, misses, and timing distribution
- Practice Tools — loop sections and slow playback for training

### 🎨 Cosmetics & Skins
- Skin Helper — preview and switch skins on the fly
- Cursor Trail Overlay — customizable cursor visuals
- Hit Sound Preview — audition hitsounds before applying

### 🏋️ Practice & Training
- Speed Trainer — adjust rate for warm-up sessions
- Pattern Drills — load custom map segments for focused practice
- Stream Trainer — short loopable maps for consistency
- Session Stats — track daily playtime and performance trends
